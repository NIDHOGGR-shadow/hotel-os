## Implemented scope

- Shorter homepage: one hero, a database-backed starting rate, dates, four room categories, dining/menu, compact discovery links and a gallery.
- Clearer room comparison and reduced repeated room-collection copy; existing dates, totals and checkout remain connected.
- Responsive private workspace with desktop side navigation and horizontally scrollable phone tabs, consistent forms and clearer page titles.
- A published, filterable twelve-item food menu with database prices. Venue pages reuse the same menu rather than separate unpriced concept menus.
- Owner/manager menu editing, dish/category/description/price/publication controls, recipe ingredient entry, stock-item entry and receipt/waste/return/adjustment recording.
- Existing guest dining request forms remain persistent staff enquiries. An instant restaurant checkout or table confirmation has not been added.

## Food price provenance

The initial twelve HOTEL food prices use the official [Lagos Continental in-room dining PDF](https://www.thelagoscontinental.com/assets/images/IRD-Price-Menu.pdf) as a benchmark. That published document is older; this is not a verification of today's competitor prices. Generic dish labels and empty descriptions were chosen for HOTEL. The configured prices range from NGN 6,000 to 40,000 and are editable in the owner's workspace. Competitor service charges, taxes, hours, portions and allergy claims were not imported.

The menu configuration creates no food sale or ingredient stock. No operational stock quantities, recipes or restaurant orders were invented. Physical counts, cash/settlement reconciliation, signed day closes, purchasing and full restaurant/front-desk action screens remain further operational work.

The owner requested direct production publication without tests or trial transactions. Source and database metadata were reviewed; no automated suite, trial booking, food order, payment or invitation was executed. Read-only reference and guest-page observations do not establish a completed paid workflow or signed-in staff workflow.
