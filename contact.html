BEFORE YOU JOIN US

A few helpful details.

How do I reserve a room?

Choose your dates and guest count on our reservations page, select an available room and review the full stay price. Add your contact details and continue to Paystack for payment.

When is my reservation confirmed?

After payment, return to the confirmation page. It displays your reservation status once payment has been verified and your room recorded. Keep your payment reference for your arrival and any questions.

Can I arrange dining or an event?

Yes. Send your preferred date and party size through dining or events. Our team will follow up with arrangements and confirmation.

What if I need to change or cancel my stay?

Contact our team with your reservation or payment reference. We will explain the arrangements that apply to your booking before making a change.

Can you help with arrival directions or transport?

Send your arrival plans through guest services. Our team can help with directions and discuss transport arrangements.

Who sees my enquiry?

Your message and contact details go to the property team handling your request. Please keep passwords and payment card details out of messages.
